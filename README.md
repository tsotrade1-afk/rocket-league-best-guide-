1. From SARPBC to Season 24
Rocket League is the modern successor to Supersonic Acrobatic Rocket-Powered Battle-Cars
(SARPBC). Psyonix says SARPBC first came to PlayStation 3 in October 2008; Rocket League launched
on 7 July 2015. [1] The premise remained simple and unusually durable: rocket-powered cars play
football-like matches with one ball, but the resulting game is a physics-driven sport rather than a
conventional racing or football game.
Psyonix announced that it was joining the Epic Games family on 1 May 2019. The announcement
described an expected closing window in late May or early June, planned an Epic Games Store
release, and confirmed that Steam owners would retain access. The announcement date is not the
same thing as a documented closing date. [2]
Full cross-platform matchmaking went live on 14 January 2019 for PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch,
Xbox One, and Steam, across Casual, Competitive, and Extra Modes. [3] Rocket League then became
free to play on 23 September 2020 at 8 a.m. PDT / 3 p.m. UTC, alongside its Epic Games Store launch.
The transition added Epic account linking, Legacy Status, tournaments, and challenges, and removed
the Solo Standard playlist. [4]
What “free to play” changed
Free-to-play progression uses an Epic Games Account and a selected Primary Platform. Rank, Rocket
Pass progress, XP, and most inventory can be shared across linked platforms, but Credits and Esports
Tokens remain platform-specific; platform-exclusive items and several licensed or ultimate-DLC
categories have exceptions. Current support documentation should take precedence over launch-era
wording when the two differ. [5], [6]
The currently listed supported platforms are PC, PlayStation 4/5, Xbox One/One S/Series X|S, and
Nintendo Switch. The official play page links Epic PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo storefronts.
[7], [8] macOS and old Linux/SteamOS online support was removed: Psyonix’s January 2020
announcement said the final patch in March would disable online functionality while leaving local
and split-screen play. Current Epic support says Steam Deck/Linux can be played but macOS cannot.
